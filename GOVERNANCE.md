# Governance

teigeodata is a Teispace open-source project.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** decide the roadmap, review and merge, cut releases, and enforce the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Area owners** (listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS)) review changes in their area:
  a crate, a binding, or a country's data.
- **Contributors** propose changes by issues and pull requests.

## Decisions

Day-to-day decisions are made in pull requests by the owners of the area. Decisions that shape
the format, the public API, the data model or the data licences go through an RFC (an issue
with the `RFC` label) and are recorded as ADRs in `docs/03-decisions/`. Maintainers decide when
consensus is not reached, and give their reasons in writing.

## Becoming an owner

Sustained, high-quality contributions to an area lead to an invitation to own it. A country's
data owners are ideally people who live there or know it well.
