A GPS fix to a place

Turn a latitude and longitude into "331 m SE of Narayanhiti Royal Palace, Kathmandu, Bagmati": the nearest places with distance and bearing, and the admin units that hold the point.

A phone reports a latitude and longitude; the user wants to read where they are. Two queries answer it: reverse gives what is near the point, and admin units at a point gives what holds it. Together they make a label such as "331 m SE of Narayanhiti Royal Palace · Kathmandu, Bagmati".

Find the nearest places

reverse gives the places nearest the point, nearest first, each with meters and bearing (degrees clockwise from north, from the place to the point).

The first row is the "near" part of the label. To say "north-east of", turn the bearing into one of eight directions: ["N", "NE", "E", "SE", "S", "SW", "W", "NW"][round(bearing / 45) % 8].

Find the units that hold the point

admin_at tests the point against the admin boundaries and answers every unit it is inside, country first. Near a border, the answer is the side the point is on, not the nearest centre.
