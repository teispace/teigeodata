Reverse geocoding

From a point to the places near it: the nearest few, everything within a radius, and the places around a place.

Three ways to ask what is near, each nearest first with its distance and bearing:

Every example ran

Each snippet is cut from one program per language, the tour, which CI runs on the released pack world-np-lite.tgd (data-2026.11.14.3) and checks line by line against the output shown. The output is the same in every language. Pick your language once: every page remembers it.

Reverse geocode a point

reverse gives the places nearest a point, nearest first, each with meters (the great-circle distance) and bearing (degrees clockwise from north, from the place to the point: "3.2 km at 45° from Pokhara").

Results are Near values: the place, meters and bearing_deg, written into a Vec you reuse.

For the admin units that hold a point (its district, province and country) use admin_at below; reverse answers with what is near it.

Find everything within a radius

within_radius gives the places within a distance of a point, nearest first, with the same meters and bearing columns, at most limit of them.

Find places near a place

nearby is reverse geocoding from a place you already have: the places nearest it, itself left out.
